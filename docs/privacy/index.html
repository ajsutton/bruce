Bruce

Privacy

Bruce connects to your household’s Home Assistant and OpenClaw services. It does not include advertising or tracking software.

Accounts and household information

Bruce keeps connection credentials and its OpenClaw device key in your device’s Keychain. Home Assistant supplies household readings and receives control requests. Cloudflare Access authenticates your OpenClaw account. OpenClaw receives the account and device identifiers needed to authorise your connection and show the agents you can access.

Voice conversations

The microphone is used only after you start a voice conversation and grant permission. Audio travels to the voice provider configured by the OpenClaw gateway. The current household setup uses OpenAI GPT-Live. OpenClaw receives conversation transcripts and coordinates the agent’s work. An established call can continue while you use another app or lock your phone. Mute stops outgoing microphone audio; End closes the voice connection.

Audio, conversation content, and account identifiers are processed to provide the requested conversation. OpenClaw retains conversation history according to the gateway operator’s settings, even though Bruce has no history screen. The voice provider’s own account terms and retention settings also apply; Bruce does not promise that provider processing is transient. Ask the household gateway operator about access, retention, and deletion.

Your choices and requests

You can revoke microphone permission in your device’s system Settings at any time. End closes the current voice connection. To revoke account access or delete retained conversations, contact your household gateway operator. You can also contact the Bruce developer with a privacy request for help reaching the responsible operator. Do not include credentials, transcripts, or other personal information in a public issue.

Service providers must protect shared information to a standard at least equivalent to this policy, including protecting account and voice information and limiting processing to the agreed purposes. The gateway operator is responsible for selecting provider terms and settings that meet those protections and for responding to access and deletion requests.

On your device

Bruce does not create a local recording or a persistent voice-history database. Agent photos are cached in memory for display. Ending a call releases the microphone and audio connection; it does not delete the gateway’s conversation history or cancel previously accepted agent work.
